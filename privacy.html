Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Welcome to Papaya Peg. This privacy policy explains how Matthew Fadoul ("I", "me", or "the Developer") handles data within the Papaya Peg iOS application.

1. Overview

Papaya Peg is designed to be a privacy-focused game. I do not require a Papaya Peg account, and I do not collect or store your personal identity, name, or contact information on any servers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

What is stored: Papaya Peg stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device using standard iOS storage. I do not have access to this local data, and it is not sent to any server operated by me. Depending on your Apple device settings, local app data may be included in Apple-managed device backups or iCloud backups. If you choose to enable Apple Game Center features, or if iCloud Sync is available and active, the app may submit the gameplay information needed for those features to Apple, as described below.

Specifically, the app stores:

  • Current and saved game state for each puzzle variant
  • High score records and recent completed-game results
  • Daily Challenge and Weekly Challenge history and progress
  • Quest progress and selected Quest stages
  • Board-specific play settings
  • Papaya currency balance, local Hint/Undo credit balances, and identifiers for already-processed in-app purchases (kept only to avoid granting the same purchase twice)
  • App preferences, including sound, haptics, launch tips, Challenge Notification settings, and Game Center opt-in state

No game state, high score, Daily Challenge, Weekly Challenge, Quest, currency, or settings data is transmitted to any server operated by me. Some of this data may instead be transmitted to your own private iCloud database if iCloud Sync is available and active, as described in Section 3. The app does not include developer-operated analytics or tracking.

3. Optional iCloud Sync

Papaya Peg may optionally sync certain gameplay data across your devices using Apple's CloudKit framework, stored in your own private iCloud database associated with your Apple ID. iCloud Sync is separate from Game Center and needs no additional sign-in beyond already being signed into iCloud on your device.

When iCloud Sync is available and active, Papaya Peg may store and merge your Shared Papaya balance (added by in-app purchases), classic board results, Daily and Weekly Challenge results, Quest progress, and in-progress active games, so the same information is available on your other Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account. This data is stored only in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access through your Apple ID; I do not operate this database and cannot access, view, or download its contents.

iCloud Sync is optional. Papaya Peg works fully offline without it, and not being signed into iCloud, or choosing not to use iCloud Sync, does not affect local play. Settings shows current sync status and, on the rare occasion two of your devices have different in-progress games for the same board, lets you choose which one to keep.

4. Optional Apple Game Center Features

Papaya Peg may offer optional Apple Game Center features, including leaderboards and awards (Game Center achievements). These features are opt-in. If you choose not to enable Game Center features, Papaya Peg does not submit your scores or award progress to Game Center.

If you choose to enable Game Center features and are signed in to Game Center with your Apple ID, Papaya Peg may submit gameplay information needed for those features to Apple through Apple's GameKit APIs. This may include scores, leaderboard entries, award or achievement progress, and related Game Center player identifiers. Apple processes and displays Game Center information according to your Apple account and Game Center privacy settings.

I do not operate Game Center, receive your Apple ID contact information, or maintain a separate server copy of your Game Center data. You can manage Game Center availability and sharing through iOS and Game Center settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Papaya Peg offers optional consumable in-app purchases ("Papaya packs") that add Papayas, the app's in-game currency, to your account. All purchases are billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and your Apple ID. I do not collect, process, see, or store your payment card, billing address, or other financial or payment information at any point; Apple handles this directly under Apple's own privacy and payment terms.

To avoid granting the same purchase more than once, Papaya Peg keeps a local record of already-processed purchase transaction identifiers on your device (and, if iCloud Sync is active, as part of the private iCloud data described in Section 3). This record does not include your name, payment method, or any other Apple ID account details.

6. Advertising

Papaya Peg may display banner advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-app reward, such as extra Hint or Undo credits, and are never shown automatically or without your action. The app also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and manage advertising consent or privacy choices where required.

Google may process device, ad delivery, consent, and interaction information to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads according to Google's policies. You can learn more about how Google handles information at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Papaya Peg's first ad-enabled release is configured for non-tracking advertising. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

7. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Papaya Peg does not request ATT permission and does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. If a future version adds tracking-based advertising, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before or alongside that release.

8. Children's Privacy

Papaya Peg does not knowingly collect personal information from users of any age. The app is suitable for all ages. Optional Game Center features are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple account, parental control, and Game Center settings. In-app purchases are billed through the purchaser's Apple ID; parents can use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls to manage a child's ability to make purchases.

9. Changes to This Policy

If future versions of the app introduce new features that affect data handling, this policy will be updated before or alongside that release.
